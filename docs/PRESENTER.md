# Five-minute presenter script

Say this is a fictional contractor. Prices are demo figures. The technicians’ visits are in English even when the receptionist speaks Spanish.

1. **Large screen.** Sign in to the owner dashboard. Point at the integration banner: simulated, because the provider accounts were not connected in this environment. Say live mode uses the same screens when credentials are present.
2. **Phone.** Open `/call`. Choose Auto, then Avery. Start **simulated browser voice**. Say it is browser voice, not a telephone call. The missed-call forward is the replay you are about to run, labeled simulated.
3. **Large screen.** Run **English call**. The journey shows the missed call, the continuation email under stage 1, and the booking. Open the email preview and the tool trace. The continuation status says simulated, not delivered.
4. Open the continuation link from the preview on the phone. Upload the sample photo or any JPG. Return to the dashboard and show the attachment banner: fictional demo photo, not a diagnosis.
5. Click **Mark demo service completed**. Show the invoice banner `DEMO: NO PAYMENT DUE` and the PDF. The review invitation appears as its own stage.
6. Click **Advance demo clock to reminder**. Read the banner: the clock jumped. A real 48 hours did not elapse. One reminder appears. Click it again and show that a second reminder was not created.
7. If you have time, run **Spanish call** or **English, then Spanish**. Turn on **Show English translation**. The Spanish lines stay on screen. Names, the address, and `$89.00` / `$225.00` are unchanged.

Do not tell the audience a technician was dispatched, that a payment was collected, or that a public review was posted.
