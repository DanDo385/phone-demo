# Sample customer scripts

These match the simulated replay. Substituting the designated test email is required before any real send. `example.com` addresses are not delivered.

## English

- Hi, the shutoff valve under my kitchen sink is leaking. Can someone come tomorrow?
- Jordan Hale
- 772-555-0148
- jordan.hale@example.com
- 1842 SW Palmetto Trace, Port St. Lucie, Florida
- Yes, that's correct.
- Yes, please email me a link so I can upload a photo.
- Please book the earliest morning appointment tomorrow.

## Spanish

- Elena Varga
- 772-555-0194
- elena.varga@example.com
- 910 SW Tradition Parkway, Tradition, Florida
- Sí, es correcto.
- Sí, por favor envíeme un enlace para subir una foto.
- Reserve la primera hora de la mañana.

## Switch during the call

Start in English with the leak and the name Jordan Hale. Then say **Español, por favor** and give the phone, email, and address. Confirm in Spanish and ask for the link. Then say **Can we continue in English?** and book the earliest morning appointment.

The inquiry id, the address, the estimate, and the selected appointment stay on the same request.

## Unsupported language

Pouvez-vous parler français?

The receptionist should stay in the current language, say that only English and Spanish are available, and offer those or a follow-up from Alex Rivera.
